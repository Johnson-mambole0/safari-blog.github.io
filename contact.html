Before you get in touch

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

For peak season (June–October) we recommend booking 4–6 months in advance, especially for Kilimanjaro climbs which have limited capacity. Off-peak travel can often be arranged with 4–6 weeks notice. Reach out as early as you can — it's never too early to start planning.
